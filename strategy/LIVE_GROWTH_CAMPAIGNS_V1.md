# LIVE GROWTH CAMPAIGN — V1

Status: ACTIVE TEST LAYER
Protection rule: Never modify or delete existing scheduled posts.

## Campaign 01 — Cash Proof
Audience: acquisition buyers, owners evaluating a transaction, brokers/advisors
Offer: free first-look Cash Proof
CTA: Request a Cash Proof
Core promise: Does the cash match the story?
Qualification: buyer/seller, company size, transaction stage, urgency

## Campaign 02 — CFO Financial Health Review
Audience: owner-led SMB / lower-middle-market companies
Offer: structured financial health review
CTA: Book a financial health review
Core promise: find the financial constraint before it becomes an operating problem

## Campaign 03 — Deal Readiness Check
Audience: owners considering sale/refinancing/acquisition
Offer: transaction-readiness diagnostic
CTA: Request a Deal Readiness Check
Core promise: identify what a lender/buyer will challenge before they do

## Campaign 04 — OCTA Founding Access
Audience: owners, CFOs, controllers, finance professionals
Offer: free founding access / feedback cohort
CTA: Request founding access
Rule: no fake scarcity; collect role, revenue band, use case and feedback

## Offer architecture
1. Free diagnostic
2. High-value bonus deliverable
3. OCTA founding access
4. Bundled CFO + diligence value
5. Time-bound service credit
6. Percentage discount only if testing proves it improves qualified conversion without damaging positioning

## Discount tests
A. No discount — premium diagnostic
B. Diagnostic + complimentary 30-minute review
C. Diagnostic + transaction-readiness roadmap
D. CFO engagement + included first OCTA setup/review
Do not lead with generic 10/20/30% off.
