# TaxBooks.CFO Commercial Offer Engine — v1

## Objective
Turn content attention into qualified demand for CFO advisory, financial diligence, and OCTA.

## Core offers

### 1. Cash Proof
Audience: owners and acquisition buyers.
Promise: Does the cash match the story?
Deliverable: bank deposits vs reported revenue, timing/gap flags, investigation questions, and a go / dig deeper / walk away framing.
CTA: Request a Cash Proof.

### 2. CFO Financial Health Review
Audience: business owners.
Purpose: identify financial blind spots before a major decision.
Review: cash, margins, working capital, debt service, concentration, forecasting, and management reporting.
CTA: Book a financial health review.

### 3. Deal Readiness Check
Audience: owners preparing for sale, refinancing, or acquisition.
Review: earnings quality, working capital, debt/cash flow, customer concentration, reporting, tax/accounting readiness.
CTA: Request a Deal Readiness Check.

### 4. Buyer Cash Proof
Audience: acquisition buyers.
Promise: Before you trust the P&L, test the cash.
CTA: Request a Cash Proof.

### 5. OCTA Founding Access
Audience: owners, CFOs, and finance professionals.
Positioning: founding access, not cheap software.
Use free access as the acquisition mechanism while collecting use case, company size, role, and feedback. Never manufacture scarcity.

## Promotion hierarchy
Prefer value creation over discounting:
1. Free diagnostic
2. Bonus deliverable
3. Founding access
4. Bundled value
5. Time-bound service credit
6. Percentage discount only when justified

## Bundles

### CFO + Deal Readiness
CFO financial health assessment + reporting/forecasting review + transaction-readiness assessment + prioritized remediation roadmap.

### Buyer Diligence + Cash Proof
Cash Proof + diligence findings + red-flag register + management questions.

### CFO + OCTA
Human CFO judgment plus continuous financial visibility. Never position OCTA as replacing the CFO.

## Campaigns

### Cash Week
Profit vs cash, working capital, DSO, inventory, runway, EBITDA-to-cash, then Cash Proof CTA.

### Deal Table Week
Buyer questions, lender questions, QoE, add-backs, working-capital peg, debt capacity, valuation risk, then Deal Readiness Check.

### Financial Reset
Close quality, forecasting, tax planning, cash, debt, KPIs, next-year planning, then CFO Financial Health Review.

### Founding OCTA Cohort
Financial blind spots, forecasting, cash visibility, decision intelligence, product demonstrations, then founding access.

## Discount rules
Never default to generic percentage-off messaging. If a discount is tested, record original price, discounted price, percentage, reason, campaign, expiration, leads, conversion, revenue, margin, and retention.

## Funnel
CONTENT -> EDUCATIONAL ASSET -> DIAGNOSTIC OFFER -> QUALIFICATION -> DISCOVERY CALL -> CFO / DILIGENCE / OCTA -> FOLLOW-UP -> REVENUE

## Qualification
Capture role, company, industry, revenue band, employee band, current finance setup, reason for inquiry, transaction status, urgency, and opportunity type.

## Measurement
Every offer gets a campaign ID. Track impressions, engagement, clicks, landing visits, form starts, submissions, qualified leads, meetings, proposals, wins, revenue, CAC where spend exists, and conversion by audience and content pillar.

## Experimentation
Test offer language, diagnostic vs bonus value, audience, CTA, and landing-page framing. Optimize for qualified conversion and revenue, not likes alone.

## Brand guardrails
Never use fake scarcity, fabricated deadlines, fabricated discounts, guaranteed financial outcomes, or unsupported savings claims. Promotions should make the offer easier to accept, not make TaxBooks.CFO look cheap.
